Welcome to the Asteroid Tracker

Explore asteroids passing near Earth in 3D.

Use your mouse to rotate, zoom, and click on asteroids for more information.
Click outside the details box twice or select the X in the top-right corner to close it.

Tip: Left-click and drag to pan around the scene, allowing you to rotate around a different point.

Use the filter panel to select the date range you want to view.

Please note: selecting a larger range will generate more asteroids, which may cause performance issues on lower-end machines.

Tip: Dates are automatically the previous 30 Days.

Use the filter panel to refine which asteroids are visible.

Filter by diameter, speed, or closest approach distance.
You can also manually enter values that exceed the slider limits.

Tip: The maximum number of asteroids that can be displayed is 500.
